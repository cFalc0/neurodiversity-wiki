# Welcome to the Neurodiversity Wiki

A community resource covering neurodiversity — the natural variation in human brain function and behavioral traits. This wiki explores ADHD, autism, learning differences, and the social and emotional experiences that often come with them.

This wiki is a growing collection of articles written to inform, validate, and support neurodivergent people and those who know and love them.

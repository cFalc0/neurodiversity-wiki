## MissUnderstood: ADHD and Oversharing
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MvbgKqAXX-s
- **Channel:** MissUnderstood: The ADHD in Women Channel (by Understood.org)
- **Also on:** [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/0JdQRXd6RIMWXkNZxeRjJu) | [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/adhd-and-oversharing/id1751325114?i=1000691005088)

- **Summary:** Explores why oversharing is so common for women with ADHD — framed as an automatic, often involuntary response rooted in impulsivity and emotional dysregulation. Covers strategies to better manage the urge to share too much information. Part of the "ADHD and..." series.
- **Key themes:** Impulsivity, emotional dysregulation, social awareness, women with ADHD, strategies
